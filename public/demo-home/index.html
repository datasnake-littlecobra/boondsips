Who it's for

For people who grew up with salt on their fruit.

If a squeeze of lime and a pinch of salt on mango, melon or cucumber was a normal afternoon at your house, this is for you. It is also for anyone who wants a drink that tastes like something, without the sugar bomb. Made for adults.

01

You grew up with it

Nimbu pani, aam panna, chaas, salted plum, chile-lime on fruit. You know the taste. We could not find it in a US store either.

02

You like less sweet

Tired of drinks that are mostly sugar? Ours has 4 g (a target, not final) and a little salt. You taste fruit, not syrup.

03

You're always out the door

One stick and a bottle of water. It fits a pocket, a tote or a carry-on, so the flavor goes where you go.

Why salt and fruit

Salt on fruit is not a trend. In many homes it is simply how fruit gets eaten. We could not find that taste in a US store, so we made it a drink.

How it works

Three moves. No blender.

01

Tear

One stick fits a pocket, a carry-on or a glove box.

02

Pour

Into about 12 oz of water.

03

Stir

Stir until it dissolves.
