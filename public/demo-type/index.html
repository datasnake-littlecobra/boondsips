From the founder

Some tastes call you home.

I grew up in India, where a drink could be sweet and salty at the same time. Living in America, I couldn't find that taste anywhere.

So I'm making it. Boond is a salty-sweet drink that brings a few kitchens, and a few cultures, into the same glass.

We're still testing, and it isn't for sale yet. Vote for the flavor we should make first, and tell us what yours reminds you of.

Prabhakar, founder of Boond

    Who it's for

    For people who grew up with salt on their fruit.

    If a squeeze of lime and a pinch of salt on mango, melon or cucumber was a normal afternoon at your house, this is for you. It is also for anyone who wants a drink that tastes like something, without the sugar bomb. Made for adults.

    01

    You grew up with it

    Nimbu pani, aam panna, chaas, salted plum, chile-lime on fruit. You know the taste. We could not find it in a US store either.

    02

    You like less sweet

    Tired of drinks that are mostly sugar? Ours has 4 g (a target while we test) and a little salt. You taste fruit, not syrup.

    03

    You're always out the door

    One stick and a bottle of water. It fits a pocket, a tote or a carry-on, so the flavor goes where you go.

    How it works

    Three moves.
    No blender.

    01

    Tear

    One stick fits a pocket, a carry-on or a glove box.

    02

    Pour

    Into about 12 oz of water.

    03

    Stir

    Stir until it dissolves.

    Questions

    Good questions.

    What is Boond?

    Boond is a salty-sweet, fruit-flavored drink with a pinch of sea salt and fiber, and we're still testing it. We're in our taste validation phase.

    Is it for sale yet?

    No. It isn't for sale yet. Join the waitlist and vote for a flavor, and we'll tell you what happens next.

    When does it launch?

    We don't have a launch date yet. We'd rather tell you when we're sure. Joining the list is the best way to hear about it.

    Which flavors are you making?

    Six are on the table: cucumber mint, watermelon, mango, guava, plum and lemon. We're deciding which to make first, and your vote helps decide.

    What do the numbers on this page mean?

    They are targets while we test: about 4 g fiber, 4 g sugar, about 200 mg sodium and about 25 calories per serving. They can change before launch, and we'll show the real label when the recipe is final.

    What do you do with my email and my story?

    Your email is only for Boond updates: taste test news, launch news and our newsletter. You can unsubscribe in one tap from any email. We use stories to plan flavors, and we won't publish a story or your name without asking you first. To see or delete what we have about you, write to hello@boondsips.com.

    Where will it be available?

    We're planning to start in the United States. We don't have a shop or stores yet, and we'll share that here and by email when we do.
