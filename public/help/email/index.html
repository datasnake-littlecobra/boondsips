Can't find our email?

Emails from Boond come from hello@boondsips.com. A new sender can land in Spam, Junk or Promotions at first. It's easy to fix, and it only takes a minute.

Quick fix

  1. Look in the other folders. Check Spam or Junk, and any tabs such as Promotions or Updates. Search your email for boondsips.
  2. Tell your email we're wanted. Open our email and choose the button that says Not spam or Not junk. In some apps it's under a menu with three dots. This moves our email to your inbox and tells your email app you want these messages.
  3. Add us to your contacts. Save hello@boondsips.com as a contact.
  4. Reply to one of our emails, even with just "thanks." You can write back to any email from us.

The names of buttons and menus differ between email apps and change over time, so yours may look a little different.

Still nothing?

Check the address

Make sure you typed the same address you use for that inbox. If you signed in or joined with a typo, join again with the right address.

Wait a few minutes, then look again

Emails can take a few minutes to arrive. Look in the folders above before you ask for another one.
